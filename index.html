Know your chimney

Five things every Toronto homeowner should know.

Your chimney is part of your home’s safety system — it vents smoke and combustion gases out of your living space. No fear tactics: drag the 3D model and tap each part — see what it does and why it matters.
Water is masonry’s worst enemy. It enters through small gaps — then freeze-thaw cycles do the damage.
Burning wood leaves creosote in the flue. Removing it keeps the chimney venting the way it should.
Toronto winters are hard on brick. A chimney serviced before the season is one less thing to think about.
Most problems are invisible from the ground. A proper look finds small issues while they’re still small.

How often should a chimney be cleaned?

Most wood-burning chimneys benefit from a yearly cleaning, but it really depends on how much you burn, what you burn, and the chimney’s condition. After seeing yours, we can recommend an honest schedule — sometimes that means less often, not more.

How do I know if my chimney needs repair?

Common signs: cracked or spalling brick, white staining, rust on the cap or damper, water in the firebox, smoke drafting poorly, or debris falling into the fireplace. An inspection confirms what’s actually going on before you spend anything.

Do you provide chimney inspections?

Yes — on their own or together with a cleaning. You get findings explained in plain language, with photos, so you can see exactly what we see.

Can you repair chimney masonry?

Yes. Brick replacement, repointing (tuckpointing), and crown work above the roofline — matched as closely as possible to your home’s existing brick.

Do you install chimney caps?

Yes. Caps keep water, animals and debris out of the flue, and they’re sized to your specific flue — not one-size-fits-all.

Do you service gas fireplaces?

Tell us about your setup when you leave your details. We’ll confirm whether it’s a fit before anything is booked — no surprises on arrival.

We serve Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, York, East York, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Markham, Thornhill, Mississauga, Brampton, Concord and surrounding communities. Not sure? Send your postal code and we’ll confirm.

How much does chimney cleaning cost?

It depends on the chimney’s condition and access — so we don’t give blind numbers online. The inspection visit is free, the technician gives you an exact price on-site, and you approve it before any work begins.

How quickly can you come?
