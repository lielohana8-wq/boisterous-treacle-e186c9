WATER

Water is masonry’s worst enemy. It enters through small gaps — then freeze-thaw cycles do the damage.

CREOSOTE

Burning wood leaves creosote in the flue. Removing it keeps the chimney venting the way it should.

WINTER

Toronto winters are hard on brick. A chimney serviced before the season is one less thing to think about.

INSPECTION
