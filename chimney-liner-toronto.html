THE STEPS
01

Assess & size

Existing flue inspected and cleaned; liner diameter matched to the fireplace, stove or furnace.

02

Install

Liner fed down from the roof and connected at the appliance.

03

Seal

Top plate and cap sealed at the crown; bottom connection secured.

04

Test

Draft verified with the appliance before we call it done.
