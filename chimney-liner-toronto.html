What does a chimney liner actually do?

It carries heat and combustion gases safely up and out, protecting the surrounding masonry and your home’s structure. It needs to be intact — full stop.

How do I know my liner is failing?

Tile fragments in the firebox, poor draft, visible cracks, or a flagged home-sale inspection. Our free inspection confirms the condition with photos.

Stainless steel or clay?

Stainless is what we install for relining: durable, correctly sized, and suitable for most appliances. We’ll explain the honest trade-offs for your setup.

Does liner size really matter?

Yes — an oversized or undersized liner drafts poorly. Sizing to the appliance is most of the job.

How long does a stainless liner last?

Decades when properly installed and maintained. We show you the condition at each visit so there are no surprises.
