Questions About Chimney Maintenance

Straight answers about chimney cleaning. Anything else — call us or send a work order.

How often should a chimney be maintained?

For most wood-burning chimneys, once a year — ideally before the season. After seeing yours we’ll recommend an honest schedule; sometimes that means less often.

What does a maintenance visit include?

A sweep and check: flue, damper, cap, crown, flashing, moisture signs — with photos logged year over year so you can see the trend.

When is the best time to book?

Late summer and early fall, before the first cold snap fills the calendar. Spring works too — problems found then are cheapest to fix.

Do you service rentals and multi-unit properties?

Yes. Leave the property details and we’ll sort scheduling and access with you or your tenants.

Is maintenance really worth it?

Catching a hairline crown crack costs a fraction of rebuilding a water-damaged stack. Maintenance is how chimneys stay boring — in the good way.
