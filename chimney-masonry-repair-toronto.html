What is tuckpointing?

Cutting back failing mortar joints and refilling them properly — struck to match the original profile, not smeared over the brick face.

Can you match the brick on an older home?

Matching is the craft. We source and blend as closely as possible so the repair disappears into the original work.

When is a rebuild needed instead of repair?

When brick and joints have deteriorated past the point of repointing — usually above the roofline. The free inspection tells you honestly which side of that line you’re on.

What season is best for masonry work?

Mortar needs workable temperatures, so most masonry runs spring through fall. Book early — the season fills up.

How long does repointing last?

Done properly, decades. Water management (crown, cap, flashing) is what protects the work — we check all three while we’re up there.
