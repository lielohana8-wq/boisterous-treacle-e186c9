FAQ

Questions we hear every week

Straight answers about chimney cleaning. Anything else — call us or send a work order.

Do I really need a chimney cap?

If the flue is open to the sky, water, snow, animals and debris are getting in. A cap is the cheapest protection a chimney can get.

Which cap is right for my chimney?

Depends on your flue size, count and fuel type. We measure on-site and recommend the right fit — single, multi-flue or custom.

Will a cap stop squirrels and raccoons?

A properly installed cap with screening keeps animals out. If something’s already nesting inside, we deal with that first.

Will a cap affect drafting?

A correctly sized cap won’t hurt draft — a wrong one can. That’s why we size to the flue instead of guessing.

How long does installation take?

Usually a single short visit once the right cap is on hand. We confirm timing when we call you back.
