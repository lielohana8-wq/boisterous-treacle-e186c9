How do I know if my chimney needs repair?

Debris in the firebox, cracked or spalling brick, white staining, rust, or smoke where it shouldn’t be. The free inspection confirms what’s actually going on before you spend anything.

Can you match my existing brick and mortar?

Yes — as closely as possible, especially on older Toronto homes. Matching is part of the job, not an extra.

Is chimney damage urgent?

Sometimes. Water and freeze-thaw make small cracks grow, so early repair is usually far cheaper. We’ll tell you honestly what’s urgent and what can wait.

How much does chimney repair cost?

It depends entirely on the damage — so we don’t give blind numbers online. The inspection is free and you approve the exact on-site price before any work begins.

Do you repair fireboxes and dampers?

Yes — firebox mortar joints and panels, and dampers that are stuck, rusted or missing.
