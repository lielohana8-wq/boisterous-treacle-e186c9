What Every Toronto Homeowner Should Know

Four things we explain on almost every visit.

Your Chimney Is A Safety System

It carries smoke and combustion gases out of your living space. When it works, you never think about it.

Water Is Masonry’s Worst Enemy

It gets in through small gaps — then Toronto’s freeze-thaw cycles do the damage.

Creosote Builds Up Every Season

Burning wood leaves it in the flue. Removing it keeps the chimney venting the way it should.

Most Problems Can’t Be Seen From The Ground

A proper look finds small issues while they are still small — and still cheap.
